#### Model
Consider the model from Berry, Levihnson, and Pakes (1995, ECMA). We will add a random effect on unobserved product quality and assess the stability of simulation and estimation. 

This is a discrete choice problem with the following indirect utility specification. 

$$u_{ij} = β_0 x_1 β_1 - α p_j + \gamma_i ξ_j + ε_{ij} $$

Notice that that $γ$ captures an individual taste for the product quality term $ξ$, which is unobserved to the researcher.
